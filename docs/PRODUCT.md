# Product Overview: FRIMA

## What we are building
FRIMA is a secure platform to connect startups and investors. 

## Target Audience
1. **Startups (Companies)**: Register and fill out a business profile. Cannot see other startups or investors.
2. **Investors / Investment Bankers**: Browse a curated list of investment opportunities (startups). Can view financial details but not private/contact info unless granted access via a "Deal Room".
3. **Admins (FRIMA Team / Directors)**: Manage the platform. Turn startups into "Opportunities", manage users, handle CRM, view analytics, and control Deal Room permissions.

## Core Workflows
- **Opportunity Pipeline**: Startup registers -> Admin reviews & publishes -> Investor browses.
- **Deal Room**: Admin explicitly grants an investor access to confidential startup documents.
- **Execution Handoff**: If an investor commits, they are redirected to an external platform to complete the transaction.
